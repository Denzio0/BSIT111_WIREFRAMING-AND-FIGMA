THE RRL AND RRS KERNEL - Interaction Paradigms Timeline

Timeline of Interaction Paradigms

Batch Processing

Terminals submit jobs with no immediate feedback.

Time-Sharing

Multiple users share system time for simultaneous use.

Networking

Systems connected to share data and resources.

Graphical Displays

Introduction of monitors and visual interfaces.

Personal Computing

Computers accessible on individual desktops.

WIMP

Windows, Icons, Menus, Pointer interfaces.

Hypertext

Clickable text links connecting information nodes.

Multimodality

Input/output through multiple channels: voice, gesture, touch.

CSCW

Collaboration through shared systems and networks.

Web

Global network of linked documents and resources.

Agent-based

Intelligent agents act on behalf of users.

Ubiquitous Computing

Computing integrated everywhere, seamless user interaction.

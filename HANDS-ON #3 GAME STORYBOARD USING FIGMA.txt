THE GAME: PANTAS
(A Culling Game Inside Pamantasan)
The Philippine Prime Minister plans to forcibly increase the Intelligence Quotient of the youth in the Philippines. To achieve this, he combines advanced technologies such as Artificial Intelligence, Virtual Reality, Augmented Reality, and Holograms. His objective is to create an environment where intelligent and strategic students compete against one another in order to push their cognitive abilities beyond normal limits.

The setting of his experiment is The Pamantasan. Inside the university, a large-scale Culling Game is launched where selected students are required to participate. The Prime Minister believes that through extreme competition and survival-based challenges, the students' intelligence will evolve to a higher level. However, the process is unethical and dangerous. If the project succeeds, it could result in chaos and the loss of thousands or even millions of lives.

To prevent this plan from becoming a national disaster, Andrei and his friends decide to enter the Culling Game themselves. Instead of fighting only for survival, they aim to outsmart the Prime Minister and expose the truth behind his experiment.

The Rules of the Culling Game Inside PAMANTASAN

1. Declaration of Participation
Anyone who awakens their inner intelligence must declare participation within 20 hours and choose a colony: GEE, GV, GCA, GB, UAC, GL, or the Field.

2. Refusal Penalty
Failure to declare participation results in loss of consciousness and eventual death. Individuals with zero IQ are exempt.

3. Automatic Registration
Non-players who enter Pamantasan and a colony automatically become players. Those inside a barrier before the game begins may leave once. Staying or re-entering confirms participation.

4. Earning Points
Points are earned by defeating and eliminating other players through intelligence and strategy.

5. Player Value
Each student and faculty member is worth 5 points. Each outsider is worth 1 point. Points are gained upon elimination.

6. Rule Addition Privilege
A player who reaches 100 points may propose one new rule. The rule must not disrupt the game. Existing rules cannot be removed.

7. Game Master Authority
The Game Master must implement valid new rules that do not weaken the system.

8. Inactivity Penalty
If a player’s score does not change for 6 to 7 days, their consciousness and IQ are erased, resulting in death.
